# Journey Through the Unknown — treatment

Deep Space Cinema test film · 15 s · 1920×1080 · 24 fps · no voice, no subtitles, no text (as briefed).

## Structure

The user's prompt fixes the three scenes, so one structure is enough (MY-STYLES.md §5): **a single journey**. Arrival, a close look, departure.

## Logline and arc

A vast ship slides out of a planet's shadow, shows us what it is, gathers itself and is gone.
Setup (emerge) → turn (engines climb; one held breath) → ending (jump; the stars settle; an empty sky where it was).

## Benchmark

The slow establishing shots and long-lens fly-bys of large-scale space features.
- **Learn:** scale from slowness and detail density, one hard sun, near-black shadow sides, silence before a release.
- **Don't take:** any ship design, silhouette, markings, music or title.

## Beat sheet

- 0.0–1.2: fade in. The bow is already clear of the limb (the hook).
- 1.6–4.8: the hum swells as the ship clears the planet.
- 5.0: cut on a low hit. The camera runs the hull; windows and beacons blink; the engines climb.
- 10.0: cut on a hit. Behind the engines, the build: plumes lengthen, brass-like swell, the roar rises.
- 11.35–11.75: **held breath.** Near-silence, engines at peak.
- 11.75: **jump.** Boom and whoosh. The ship stretches forward and accelerates; the trail lights up; the stars stream into lines.
- 12.45: the ship vanishes into the vanishing point. Flash and shimmer.
- 12.6–13.8: the streaks retract and the stars settle. The trail fades.
- 13.8–15.0: an empty sky, a resolving D-major chord, fade to black.
